ShiftPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

The short version

  • ShiftPilot has no account, no sign-in and no analytics.
  • Your jobs, shifts, pay rates, payslip figures and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • They leave your phone only when you choose to export or back them up, and then only to the place you pick in the iOS share sheet.
  • ShiftPilot shows no adverts.
  • Subscriptions are bought through Apple and checked with RevenueCat.

Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in a database on your device: the jobs you add (name, hourly rate, pay rules, colour), your shifts (dates, times, breaks, notes, paid status), payslip amounts you enter for comparison, your take-home estimate settings (tax code, region, student loan plan, pension and other deductions), your first name if you give one, and your app settings.

We cannot see, access or recover this information. It stays until you delete it in the app (Settings, Erase all my data) or delete the app.

Exports and backups

When you choose to export a CSV or PDF report, or back up your data, the app creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you send it (for example Files, iCloud Drive, email or another app), and that service's own privacy terms apply. Restoring reads only the backup file you pick.

Reminders

If you turn on shift reminders, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Subscriptions (ShiftPilot Pro)

Payments are taken by Apple using your Apple ID. We never see your payment details.

To confirm whether ShiftPilot Pro is active on your Apple ID, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives the app's anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription transactions from Apple, your device's country/region and basic device information. It uses this only to provide the subscription service to us. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

  • Information on your device: processed by you, on your device; we do not receive it.
  • Subscription checks: to perform our contract with you (providing ShiftPilot Pro).

Children

ShiftPilot is a pay tracker for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because your app data is stored only on your phone, you can view, correct, export and delete it yourself in the app at any time. For information held by Apple or RevenueCat, you can contact them directly, or contact us at support@shiftpilot.app and we will help.

You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version in the app's listing.
