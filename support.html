ShiftPilot Support

Need help with ShiftPilot: UK Pay Tracker? Email support@shiftpilot.app. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Common questions

Where are my shifts stored?

Only on your phone. ShiftPilot has no account, so we cannot see or recover your data. Use Settings, Back up my data, to save a backup file somewhere safe such as Files or iCloud Drive.

I have a new phone. How do I move my data?

On the old phone: Settings, Back up my data, and save the file. On the new phone: install ShiftPilot, then Settings, Restore from a backup, and pick that file. Restoring replaces everything on the new phone.

How do I restore my ShiftPilot Pro subscription?

In Settings, tap See ShiftPilot Pro, then Restore purchases, while signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings, [your name], Subscriptions, ShiftPilot. Or tap Manage or cancel subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

My pay figure is different from my payslip.

ShiftPilot gives estimates from the shifts and rates you enter. Check your rates, breaks and pay rules on the job, and use Payslip check to compare. Your payslip and employer are the source of truth; ShiftPilot is not payroll or tax advice.

Reminders do not appear.

Check Settings, Notifications, ShiftPilot on your iPhone, and that reminders are on in the app.
